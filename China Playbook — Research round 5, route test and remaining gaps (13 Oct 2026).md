**5.4 in full.** The three instruments say different things, and the draft's sentence merges them.

| Instrument | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Press Note 2 (2026), DPIIT, F.No. 5(5)/2020-FDI Policy (Pt-1), 15 Mar 2026, 3 pages** | Rewrites para 3.1.1 only. (a) A land-border entity or citizen, or an investor whose **beneficial owner** is one, invests only via the Government route. (b) A later ownership change that creates such a beneficial owner needs prior approval. (c) Beneficial owner has the Prevention of Money-laundering Act meaning and the thresholds of Rule 9(3) of the PML Rules; ownership is deemed land-border if such persons can exceed those thresholds, control the investor, or exercise "ultimate effective control" over the investee.
